Quit smoking, one step at a time

Swap your cigarettes for a vape on day one, then buy a weaker e-liquid every few weeks until you're at 0 mg. Answer two questions to get your plan.

2 How fast do you want to go?

Tips

Vape like you smoked

Vape when you'd have had a cigarette. Don't puff more when the strength drops; the cravings fade within a few days.

Step too hard?

Strong cravings or irritability? Stay on that strength a week or two longer. Feeling sick or dizzy means too much nicotine: vape less.

Had a cigarette?

One slip doesn't undo your progress. Carry on with the plan as if nothing happened.

Good to know

  • The plan assumes French cigarettes (at most 1 mg of nicotine each) and e-liquid strengths sold in France: 20, 16, 12, 6, 3 and 0 mg/mL. 20 mg is usually sold as nicotine salt (sels de nicotine), which feels smoother at high strength.
  • This is a guide, not medical advice. If you're pregnant, have heart disease or take medication, ask a doctor or pharmacist first. Patches, gum and prescription medicines also help people quit, and Tabac Info Service (39 89) offers free support.
  • Vaping is far less harmful than smoking but not harmless. The goal is to stop smoking first, then stop nicotine.
  • E-liquid is poisonous if swallowed. Keep it away from children and pets.

Your step-down plan

Tell us how you smoke. You get a week-by-week plan: switch to vaping without a nicotine drop, then lower the liquid strength a little each step until you're at 0 mg. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.

Fine-tune

A cigarette delivers about 1 mg of nicotine to your blood (0.5–2 mg depending on brand and how you smoke). The EU and UK cap e-liquid at 20 mg/mL.

Using the plan

Too much nicotine?

Nausea, dizziness, headache or a racing heart mean you're getting more than you need. Vape less, or move to the next step early.

Too little?

Strong cravings, irritability or reaching for a cigarette mean the step was too big. Stay on the current step longer. Don't go back up unless you'd otherwise smoke.

A slip isn't a fail

Smoked a cigarette? Carry on with the plan. A slip doesn't undo weeks of progress; going back to daily smoking would.

Getting the strengths

Each step shows the nearest strength sold in shops, and a mix recipe: blend your starting liquid with a 0 mg version of the same flavour in the ratio shown. Use a small measuring syringe and label the bottles. If you can't mix, use the shop strength and move on when the plan reaches the next one.

Be safe

  • This is a guide, not medical advice. If you're pregnant, have heart disease or take medication, talk to a doctor or pharmacist first. Nicotine replacement (patches, gum) and prescription medicines also help people stop; you can combine them with this plan.
  • Vaping is far less harmful than smoking but not harmless. The aim is to stop smoking first, then stop nicotine.
  • E-liquid is poisonous if swallowed. Keep it away from children and pets, and buy from regulated sellers.
  • The figures are estimates. How much nicotine you absorb depends on your device, coil and puffing style, so use how you feel to adjust.
